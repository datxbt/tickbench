01

Verdict

The question Stage 0 exists to answer: which instruments can carry a cost-realistic backtest, and over which years.

XAUUSDMetal
Clean

Zero locked ticks in six years. Spread is a genuine quote and compresses across broker eras — 11.3 → 3.7 pips median from 2020 to 2025. Fully supports data-driven cost modelling.

USTECIndex
Clean outside 2024–25

Locked ticks are 0% in 2021–23 and 2026, but reach 8% in 2024 and 39% in 2025. Usable, with those two years excluded from any spread estimate.

USDJPYFX
Ask side unusable

92.7% of ticks quote bid = ask. The median spread is exactly zero in every year from 2020 to 2026. No real spread can be recovered from this feed.

EURUSDFX
Ask side unusable

97.6% of ticks quote bid = ask, the worst of the four. Non-zero spreads cluster on Sunday reopens, so the ask is only real when the spread is unusually wide.

Why the locked-tick finding matters more than it looks

A raw-spread account never has a zero spread 96% of the time. The zeros are not tight markets — they are an ask column that mostly just mirrors the bid.

Left undetected, this is the kind of defect that silently produces a beautiful backtest: an FX strategy costed off this data would trade for free, and the equity curve would look excellent right up until it met a real broker. FX cost modelling needs an external spread source — a broker spec sheet or a second feed. The bid series remains usable as a price series.

03

Continuity

Naively counting gaps makes XAUUSD and USTEC look badly broken — about 1,350 hour-plus gaps each. Almost all of them are the daily maintenance break: 1,310 of XAUUSD's start between 20:00 and 21:00 UTC with a 1.05 h median, roughly one per trading day. Separating routine downtime from real outages cuts the genuine figure by 95%.

04

Session coverage

Share of each symbol's ticks by UTC hour. This is the empirical basis for the session definitions Stage 2 will need — spread and volatility both regime-shift across these boundaries.

All four peak at 13:00–15:00 UTC, the London–New York overlap. USTEC is the most concentrated — 31% of its ticks land in those three hours, against 25% for EURUSD — and collapses to 0.5% at 21:00 during its index break.

06

What this changes downstream

Stage 0 was meant to be plumbing. The locked-tick finding makes it a scoping decision instead.

Stage 1

Deduplication is safe; spread repair is not

Duplicate timestamps are exact duplicate rows, so dropping them loses nothing. Do not, however, forward-fill or interpolate the FX ask — inventing a spread is worse than having none, because it hides the problem behind plausible numbers.

Stage 2

Two cost models, not one

XAUUSD and USTEC get an empirical spread model estimated per session and per broker era. EURUSD and USDJPY need an external spread assumption, documented as an assumption, with backtests run across a range of it rather than a single point estimate.

Stage 3

Lead with XAUUSD

It is the cleanest instrument, the densest (103k ticks a day, 283M in total) and the only one with a trustworthy spread across the whole period. Prove the research loop there before taking on an instrument whose costs are guesswork.

Open

Worth confirming with the vendor

The FX ask behaviour is consistent enough across six years to look like a feed-construction artefact rather than corruption. If a corrected FX export exists, it would restore two instruments at once.
