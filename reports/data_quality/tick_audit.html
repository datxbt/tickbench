01

Verdict

The question Stage 0 exists to answer: which instruments can carry a cost-realistic backtest, and over which years. The test is agreement with the broker's published contract specification — not whether zero spreads occur.

EURUSDFX
Agrees with spec

Observed Mon–Fri mean spread 0.039 pips against a published 0.0. The 97.6% of ticks quoting bid = ask are real: this account type compresses spread to zero and charges commission instead.

USDJPYFX
Agrees with spec

Observed 0.071 pips against a published 0.0, inside tolerance. Its zero-spread share climbed 73% → 96% since 2020, tracking a genuine market-wide spread compression.

XAUUSDMetal
Spec not on file

Feed is clean — no zero-spread ticks, median compressing 11.3 → 3.7 pips since 2020. But metals commission sits on a separate spec page, so cost here is spread-only until that is added.

USTECIndex
Spec not on file

Same gap as XAUUSD. Zero-spread ticks reach 8% in 2024 and 39% in 2025, worth confirming against the index spec rather than assuming a defect.

Commission is the cost, not spread

On an Exness Raw Spread account the majors are quoted at a genuine 0.0 pip average and the broker takes revenue as $2.5 per lot per side. Round turn, that is 0.50 pips on EURUSD and about 0.76 on USDJPY — rate-dependent, since a JPY pip is worth a variable number of dollars.

Which makes commission 93% and 91% of total round-trip cost. A cost model built on the spread distribution alone — the obvious thing to build from tick data — would understate the true cost of an FX strategy by roughly a factor of ten.

07

What this changes downstream

The corpus is sound, so Stage 0 hands downstream work a cost structure rather than a list of defects.

Stage 1

Deduplication is safe

Every duplicate timestamp is an exact duplicate row, so dropping them loses nothing. Keep the Sunday reopen and the 21:00 rollover flagged rather than filtered — they are the only places FX spread exists, and a strategy needs to be able to see them to avoid them.

Stage 2

Commission first, spread second

For FX, cost is 0.50–0.76 pips of commission plus a spread that rounds to nothing outside two windows. Model it as a constant per round turn with two exclusion windows, not as a spread distribution. Make the commission term rate-aware for USDJPY, whose pip value moves with the exchange rate.

Stage 2

Treat cost as time-varying

Spreads compressed materially over the sample — XAUUSD's median fell from 11.3 to 3.7 pips. Backtesting 2020 with today's costs flatters the result; backtesting with 2020's costs understates what a strategy would earn now. Estimate per era.

Blocked

Metals and indices specs needed

XAUUSD and USTEC commission and contract size come from separate Exness specification pages. Until those are on file, cost for the two densest and cleanest instruments in the corpus is spread-only — and on the FX evidence, spread-only is the part that matters least.
